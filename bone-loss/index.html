The bone loss guide for women

Your bones can thin before you feel it.

Bone loss speeds up about 1 year before your last period. It stays fast for about 2 years after. There is no pain and no warning. This guide shows what happens, when it happens, and the four tools Huemn uses to help you hold on to bone and muscle.

How bone works

Bone is alive.

Every day, your body breaks down old bone and builds new bone. Estrogen keeps the two jobs in balance. Testosterone and progesterone help the building side. Growth hormone and IGF-1 tell bone cells and muscle cells to grow.

Three hormones protect bone and muscle. Each one drops with age. Huemn's hormone program starts with labs and a symptom review, then builds a plan for your body.

Estrogen: replace what dropped

Estrogen slows the breakdown side. When it drops, breakdown wins. Hormone therapy gives some of it back.

33%

fewer hip fractures in women on estrogen plus progestin, versus placebo. Total fractures fell 24%. Hip bone density rose 3.7% in 3 years.
Women's Health Initiative. Cauley 2003. 16,608 women.

The Menopause Society, 2022. Hormone therapy prevents bone loss and fracture. For women under 60, or within 10 years of menopause, with no medical reason to avoid it, the benefits outweigh the risks.

Progesterone: the builder

Bone-building cells carry progesterone receptors. Progesterone supports the build side while estrogen slows the breakdown side. Progesterone falls first in perimenopause, often years before estrogen.

+1.7%

spine bone density in one year with cyclic progestin and calcium. The placebo group lost 2.0%.
Prior 1994. 61 active women with cycle changes. Randomized, 1 year.

At Huemn, progesterone is part of the women's hormone plan when labs and symptoms call for it.

Testosterone: bone and muscle

Women make testosterone too, in the ovaries and adrenal glands, and it falls through the 40s. Testosterone drives bone building and muscle growth. Muscle pulls on bone. More muscle means more load on bone, and bone gets stronger when you load it.

E + T

Adding testosterone to estradiol raised bone density more than estradiol alone over two years in postmenopausal women.
Davis 1995. 34 women. 2 years.

6.3%

more thigh muscle in one year of hormone therapy in women aged 50 to 57, versus 0.7% in controls.
Sipilä 2001. 80 women. Randomized, 1 year.

+7.5%

spine trabecular bone density in one year in men over 65 with low testosterone, versus 0.8% on placebo. Estimated spine strength rose 10.8%.
Snyder 2017. 211 men. Randomized, 1 year.

+0.9 kg

lean mass versus placebo after 3 years in men over 60. Chest-press strength and stair-climb power rose too.
Storer 2017. 256 men. Randomized, 3 years.

The largest bone trials are in men, and they show how strong the signal is. Huemn treats low testosterone in women as part of the hormone plan. Labs first. Then a plan.

Growth hormone and IGF-1 are the body's build signals for bone and muscle. Both fall with age. Growth hormone peptides ask your own pituitary to release more. All Huemn peptide protocols are prescribed by a provider and reviewed by Dr. Syed Jamal, MD.

PEMF: signal your bones

PEMF stands for pulsed electromagnetic field. The device sends magnetic pulses into your body. Bone cells respond to these signals. The FDA first approved PEMF in 1979 to help broken bones that would not heal on their own. At Huemn, you relax in the PEMF chair or on the PEMF table.
