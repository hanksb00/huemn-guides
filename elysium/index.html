What it is

Light your cells can use.

the science calls it photobiomodulation. the Elysium bed delivers it head to toe.

No UV

You do not tan. You do not burn.

Gentle warmth

You feel some warmth. The 940 nm light adds it. The real work happens inside the cell.

Not one color

Six wavelengths, from green to near-infrared.

Full body

Light from above and below at the same time.

Wavelength

The color of light, measured in nanometers (nm). Each wavelength is absorbed at a different depth.

Where it starts

It starts in your mitochondria.

light in. energy out.

Red and near-infrared light is absorbed by an enzyme in your mitochondria. Its name is cytochrome c oxidase. The cell then makes more ATP, its unit of energy. More energy supports repair and recovery.

Hamblin 2017 · AIMS Biophysics
Six wavelengths

Each one reaches a different depth.

tap a wavelength.

Wavelength in nanometers. Depths are not to scale.

The evidence

What the research shows.

human studies of red and near-infrared light.

Skin
136volunteers, ages 27 to 79. Thirty sessions, twice a week.
69%to 75% of treated faces rated improved by blinded experts. Untreated: 4%.

Collagen density rose on ultrasound. Skin roughness went down. Complexion and skin feeling improved.

Wunsch and Matuschka 2014 · Photomedicine and Laser Surgery · 136 people
Performance
+5.47repetitions with light before exercise, compared with placebo.
+4.12 slonger to exhaustion.

A meta-analysis of 13 randomized controlled trials. The most consistent results came from red and near-infrared wavelengths.

Leal-Junior 2015 · Lasers in Medical Science · 13 trials
Recovery
14studies in one meta-analysis.
CK lowerafter exercise, compared with control.

Creatine kinase (CK) is a blood marker of muscle damage. Less of it after a hard session points to less damage to repair. That is why athletes build light into their recovery days.

Machado 2020 · Clinical Journal of Sport Medicine · 14 studies

The studies used regular sessions over weeks, not one-offs. Full references are in Sources.
